Method

Source. FCC Broadband Data Collection fixed broadband availability, as-of date 30 June 2026, state files for technology code 50 (fiber to the premises), all record types (residential, business and mixed-use).

Consolidated (Fidium). Consolidated (Fidium) files as FCC provider ID 130335 (Consolidated Communications Holdings, filing under the Fidium Fiber brand); it is the only Consolidated (Fidium) filer in the data. Its fiber footprint is 1,133,938 Broadband Serviceable Locations across 17 states.

Overlap. A Consolidated (Fidium) passing counts as overbuilt when a rival reports technology 50 at the same location_id in the same filing. Cable, fixed wireless and DSL rivals are excluded — this is fiber-on-fiber only. Rival rows do not sum to the state total: one Consolidated (Fidium) location can be overbuilt by several rivals, and the state total counts each location once.

Units and identity. Counts are BSLs, not housing units — one location can be a multi-unit building. Rivals are keyed by FCC provider ID rather than brand, so a filer using more than one brand name counts once — for example AT&T / Quantum Fiber; Lincolnville Telephone Company / TIDEWATER O.P.E.N.; FirstLight Fiber / FirstLight Fiber d/b/a Oxford West Telephone Company; separate IDs stay separate even where they share a corporate parent.

Caveats. Every record under this ID carries the brand name Fidium Fiber (Consolidated's unified brand since September 2025); the legacy Consolidated Communications, Enventis, SureWest and FairPoint names no longer appear in the filing. Four unrelated filers whose names contain “Consolidated” are not included: Consolidated Telcom (ID 130337, a Dickinson, North Dakota telephone cooperative), Consolidated Companies of Nebraska (131211), Consolidated Fiber (380034, Consolidated Cooperative in Ohio) and Consolidated Smart Broadband Systems (370028).

The Kansas City network was sold to Everfast Fiber (ID 470028) in November 2022 and is not in this footprint; the Missouri (324) and Kansas (2) rows are remnants, not markets. The North Dakota, Iowa and Wisconsin rows are Enventis-legacy business fiber in Fargo, Sioux City and Superior (71–94% business-only records), so their overbuild shares describe commercial buildings rather than homes.

Built from the FCC BDC public availability files. Counts reflect what providers filed, including any reporting error, and are not audited service records.
