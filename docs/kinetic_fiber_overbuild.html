Method

Source. FCC Broadband Data Collection fixed broadband availability, as-of date 30 June 2026, state files for technology code 50 (fiber to the premises), all record types (residential, business and mixed-use).

Windstream Kinetic. Windstream Kinetic files as FCC provider ID 131413 (Uniti Group Inc., formerly Windstream Holdings; Kinetic is the residential fiber brand); it is the only Windstream Kinetic filer in the data. Its fiber footprint is 1,637,665 Broadband Serviceable Locations across 19 states.

Overlap. A Windstream Kinetic passing counts as overbuilt when a rival reports technology 50 at the same location_id in the same filing. Cable, fixed wireless and DSL rivals are excluded — this is fiber-on-fiber only. Rival rows do not sum to the state total: one Windstream Kinetic location can be overbuilt by several rivals, and the state total counts each location once.

Units and identity. Counts are BSLs, not housing units — one location can be a multi-unit building. Rivals are keyed by FCC provider ID rather than brand, so a filer using more than one brand name counts once — for example Blue Ridge Cable Technologies / PenTeleData; Optimum / Lightpath; Tri-CoGo / Tri-County Fiber Communications, LLC; separate IDs stay separate even where they share a corporate parent.

Caveats. Since 1 August 2025 the filer's parent is Uniti Group Inc. (formerly Windstream Holdings); Kinetic is its residential fiber brand. Five Uniti Fiber legacy brands filed under the same ID — Uniti Fiber GulfCo, Southern Light, Hunt Telecommunications, Contact Network and Information Transport Solutions, 6,136 business-only locations that were filed there before the merger — are enterprise fiber rather than Kinetic passings and are excluded from the footprint above. That removes Louisiana entirely (the ID has no Kinetic passings there) and trims Mississippi, Alabama and Florida slightly; the headline overbuild share moves by a tenth of a point.

Valor Telecommunications of Texas (243,959 locations in TX, OK, NM and AR) is Windstream's south-western ILEC and is included, as is Windstream Communications LLC (38,803 CLEC edge-out locations).

Built from the FCC BDC public availability files. Counts reflect what providers filed, including any reporting error, and are not audited service records.
