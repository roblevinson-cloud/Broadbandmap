Method

Source. FCC Broadband Data Collection fixed broadband availability, as-of date 31 December 2025, state files for technology code 50 (fiber to the premises), all record types (residential, business and mixed-use).

Lumos. Lumos files as FCC provider ID 130403 (Lumos Telecom, the T-Mobile / EQT joint venture, filed under T-Mobile USA's provider ID); it is the only Lumos filer in the data. Its fiber footprint is 627,129 Broadband Serviceable Locations across 9 states.

Overlap. A Lumos passing counts as overbuilt when a rival reports technology 50 at the same location_id in the same filing. Cable, fixed wireless and DSL rivals are excluded — this is fiber-on-fiber only. Rival rows do not sum to the state total: one Lumos location can be overbuilt by several rivals, and the state total counts each location once.

Units and identity. Counts are BSLs, not housing units — one location can be a multi-unit building. Rivals are keyed by FCC provider ID rather than brand, so a filer using more than one brand name counts once — for example CenturyLink / Quantum Fiber; Randolph Telephone Membership Corp. / Randolph Telephone Telecommunications Inc.; Lingo Networks / MGW Telephone; separate IDs stay separate even where they share a corporate parent.

Caveats. ID 130403 belongs to T-Mobile USA, which files its licensed fixed wireless under it nationwide; Lumos (the T-Mobile / EQT joint venture, closed 1 April 2025) has filed its fiber under the same ID since the December 2024 vintage, having filed as ID 300191 before that. Rows carrying the brand name “T-Mobile” under this ID — 36,990 locations in Kenosha WI, Rockford IL and Farmington Hills MI — are T-Mobile Fiber retail service sold over SiFi Networks' open-access fiber, not Lumos plant, and are excluded from the footprint above. In other providers' reports they appear as a separate rival labelled T-Mobile Fiber.

NorthState (Guilford, Davidson, Randolph and Forsyth counties, North Carolina) is filed under this ID and is included. Metronet, which joined a separate T-Mobile joint venture in July 2025, files under its own ID (131081) and is treated as a distinct provider; the two share no locations.

Built from the FCC BDC public availability files. Counts reflect what providers filed, including any reporting error, and are not audited service records.
