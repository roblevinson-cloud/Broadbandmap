Franchise recapture

Replacement ratio = fiber net adds ÷ absolute legacy net losses. Above 100% is the simple stabilization threshold.

Build productivity

Synthetic build yield = Δ fiber subscribers ÷ Δ fiber passings. It is not a true cohort take-rate.

Legacy pressure

Legacy leakage intensity = |Δ legacy subscribers| ÷ Δ fiber passings. Divestitures can distort it.
