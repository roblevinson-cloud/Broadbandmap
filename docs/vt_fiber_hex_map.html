About this map

Source. FCC Broadband Data Collection fixed availability, as of 31 Dec 2025, Vermont file for technology code 50 (fiber to the premises).

Homes passed. Distinct Broadband Serviceable Locations a provider reports serving with fiber, counting only residential-serviceable records (codes R and X). A multi-unit building is one location. Availability is what the provider filed, not subscriptions.

Hexes. H3 resolution 8 (about 0.74 km² on average, roughly 0.8 km across, 0.46 km from centre to corner). A hex is coloured for the selected provider passing the most homes in it, or red where two or more selected providers pass the same homes; the red shade is the share of the hex's selected-provider homes that are passed by two or more of them. Overlap is measured home by home, not hex by hex, so two providers in the same hex but on different streets do not count as overlap.

Identity. Providers are FCC provider IDs, not brand names, so one filer using several brands counts once. The two brand splits used in the national overbuild reports (T-Mobile rows filed under Lumos's ID, Uniti brands under Windstream's) do not occur in the Vermont file.

Basemap. Street tiles come from CARTO (Positron and Dark Matter, place labels drawn above the hexes; needs a free API key, 5 million tiles a month) or from OpenStreetMap's standard tiles (no key). Where a viewer blocks external images (the claude.ai artifact view does) the map falls back to county outlines and its own city labels.

Colours. The 20 largest providers have their own hue; providers ranked 21st and beyond reuse those hues in a darker or lighter step, so rely on the checklist and the hover panel, not colour alone, to identify a small provider.
