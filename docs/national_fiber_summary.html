US fiber overlap summary — 48 lower-48 states

How many homes each state's fiber providers pass, how that compares to the state's total broadband-serviceable universe (fiber penetration), how many of those fiber homes have a choice of 2, 3, or 4+ competing fiber providers, which providers overlap the most with each other, and — for the homes fiber hasn't reached yet — which incumbent LEC/ILEC/RLEC most likely holds that territory, rolled up from the per-state hex maps. Homes passed = distinct residential-serviceable locations (record types R and X); "total passings" is the same locations with any fixed technology on file (cable, fiber, or licensed fixed wireless), so fiber penetration is fiber ÷ total. Click a state row to see its ten largest providers and who overlaps them.

Note. In the June 2026 filing, Metronet Holdings (FCC provider ID 131081) reports its fiber locations as business-only (code B) in 16 states (AZ, CO, FL, IA, IL, IN, KY, MI, MN, MO, NC, NE, OH, OK, VA, WI): 2,206,393 locations, against 2,042,041 residential (R/X) homes it filed there in December 2025. Under this page's residential-only rule they are not counted, so those homes drop out of its total and overlap is understated relative to December 2025.

By LEC — likely incumbent for un-fibered homes (358)

For every home with broadband but no fiber yet, whoever files a Copper (xDSL) record there is almost always that location's incumbent LEC/ILEC/RLEC — CLECs essentially never build their own copper plant. This rolls that up nationally: 41,060,706 un-fibered homes, 20,228,030 (49.3%) matched to a copper filer. Verizon files no copper anywhere in this data, so its ILEC territory (much of DE/MD/NJ/NY/PA/VA/MA/RI/CT/WV) shows almost no match here — that is a gap in what carriers file, not evidence Verizon has abandoned those homes. The rest of the 20,832,676 un-fibered homes with no copper match either have no legacy copper plant or their carrier has stopped filing it.

Density proxy: homes per H3 resolution-8 hex (≈0.74 km² everywhere, so homes-per-hex stands in for housing density with no separate census join).
