Fiber-on-Fiber Overlap

Share of each provider's fiber passings that are also covered by another fiber provider — split by whether 0, 1, or 2+ other fiber networks report service at the same location.

Google Fiber is still the most overbuilt fiber footprint in the country (68% overlapped) — above all by AT&T (1.05M shared passings), whose filing now includes the Quantum Fiber footprint it bought from Lumen. Brightspeed (49%) is the most-overlapped incumbent telco. At the other end, the big ILECs defend mostly exclusive footprints: AT&T and Frontier are ~76% sole-fiber, Verizon 81%, Ziply 84%, and altafiber's Cincinnati footprint is 90% exclusive — the least overbuilt of the fourteen.

Why business-only filings are excluded. Altice's Lightpath unit files fiber availability at 5.4M locations across the Northeast and mid-Atlantic — 100% of them business-only records. Counting those as "fiber overlap" would roughly double Verizon's overlapped share (19.2% → 39.3%) without a single competing residential fiber passing. This analysis therefore counts only records coded residential or both (R/X), on both sides of every comparison. Altice's real Optimum FTTH overbuild still shows up — it is Verizon's #1 overlapper at 1.6M shared passings.
Metronet filed business-only in June 2026. Metronet (FCC provider ID 131081) reports its Metronet-branded fiber — 2.52M locations — as business-only (code B) in this filing; in December the same locations carried residential records. They are counted as homes here because the same locations were residential in December 2025, so Metronet's row (2,888,144 passings, 42.7% overlapped; December: 2,612,686 and 39.5%) and its overlaps with AT&T, Brightspeed and others are on the same footing as December. In the June 2026 filing, some providers recoded locations they had filed as residential in December 2025 as business-only (code B): Peoples Communications (ID 131031) 10,559; Highline Internet (ID 460005) 5,421; Etex Communications (ID 130464) 5,072; AireBeam Fiber Internet (ID 470030) 4,157; Auburn Essential Services (ID 180010) 1,855. Those locations are counted as homes here.
Lumen's fiber now files under AT&T. 2.92M of the 3.16M residential fiber passings Lumen filed in December are filed in June 2026 under AT&T's provider ID (130077), brand Quantum Fiber, following AT&T's purchase of Lumen's consumer fiber business. AT&T's row therefore includes them, and the Lumen row is only the 150,716 CenturyLink-branded fiber passings still filed under Lumen's own ID (130228). Former AT&T–Quantum overlaps now count as one provider.

The M&A market has been buying overbuilders. At Dec 31, 2025 the fourteen incumbents mostly sat at 15–35% overlap; the acquired cohort clustered at 40–70% — GoNetspeed (70%), IdeaTek (67%), Tachus (64%). The utility-like exceptions: Gateway Fiber (10.5%) and i3 Broadband (32%). Not shown: cable-hybrid acquirees (Cox → Charter, WOW! → DigitalBridge/Crestview), whose footprints are mostly HFC; Vexus, whose Metronet deal closed June 2022, outside the four-year window; and US Internet (→ Metronet, Sep 2025, ~101k passings), already absorbed into Metronet's filing ID by Dec 2025. Targets on the main list — Frontier → Verizon, Quantum Fiber → AT&T, Metronet → T-Mobile/KKR, Ziply → BCE, Consolidated → Searchlight, Windstream ↔ Uniti, Google Fiber → Stonepeak/Astound (pending) — appear in their own rows above.

Method

  • Source: FCC Broadband Data Collection fixed availability, as-of 06/30/2026 (October 2026 public release), technology code 50 (fiber to the premises), all 50 states + DC — 74.8M fiber-passed BSLs, 1,551 fiber filers.
  • Passings basis: distinct broadband-serviceable locations. A multi-unit building counts as one BSL, so MDU-heavy footprints (Hotwire, Verizon, Blue Stream) show fewer BSLs than their marketed unit passings.
  • Overlap: another distinct filing provider (any of the 1,551, not just these fourteen) reporting technology 50 at the same location_id in the same vintage. Keyed by provider_id, never brand.
  • Entity notes: Windstream = all Kinetic subsidiaries plus Uniti entities (one filing ID). Metronet includes Vexus (residential rows are Vexus only in this vintage; see note). AT&T includes Quantum Fiber from this vintage; Lumen = the CenturyLink brand left under ID 130228. altafiber excludes sister company Hawaiian Telcom (262k BSLs, same filing ID). Consolidated = Fidium's filing entity only.
  • Acquired cohort: screened to targets with 100k+ residential-capable fiber BSLs at Dec 2025 (section not updated); deal window Sept 2022 – Aug 2026 by announce or close date; majority-control changes only. Lumos = provider ID 130403 filtered to Lumos-branded records (the same ID also files T-Mobile Fiber). Empire Access = its filing entity (Empire Long Distance), which includes sister brands. Pending deals (the three T-Mobile JVs) are marked in the table.
  • Caveats: availability is provider-reported and can overstate marketable footprint. On open-access networks (e.g. UTOPIA in Utah) each retail ISP counts as a separate overlapper, which inflates 2+ counts in those areas.
