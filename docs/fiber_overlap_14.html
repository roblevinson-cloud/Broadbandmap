Fiber-on-Fiber Overlap

Share of each provider's fiber passings that are also covered by another fiber provider — split by whether 0, 1, or 2+ other fiber networks report service at the same location.

Google Fiber is the most overbuilt fiber footprint in the country (64% overlapped) — a consequence of building where AT&T already is. Brightspeed (48%) is the most-overlapped incumbent telco. At the other end, the big ILECs defend mostly exclusive footprints: AT&T and Frontier are ~78% sole-fiber, Verizon 81%, Ziply 85%, and altafiber's Cincinnati footprint is 91% exclusive — the least overbuilt of the fourteen.

Why business-only filings are excluded. Altice's Lightpath unit files fiber availability at 5.4M locations across the Northeast and mid-Atlantic — 100% of them business-only records. Counting those as "fiber overlap" would nearly double Verizon's overlapped share (18.7% → 39.1%) without a single competing residential fiber passing. This analysis therefore counts only records coded residential or both (R/X), on both sides of every comparison. Altice's real Optimum FTTH overbuild still shows up — it is Verizon's #1 overlapper at 1.6M shared passings.

The M&A market has been buying overbuilders. The fourteen incumbents above mostly sit at 15–35% overlap; the acquired cohort clusters at 40–70% — GoNetspeed (70%), IdeaTek (67%), Tachus (64%). The utility-like exceptions: Gateway Fiber (10.5%) and i3 Broadband (32%). Not shown: cable-hybrid acquirees (Cox → Charter, WOW! → DigitalBridge/Crestview), whose footprints are mostly HFC; Vexus, whose Metronet deal closed June 2022, outside the four-year window; and US Internet (→ Metronet, Sep 2025, ~101k passings), already absorbed into Metronet's filing ID by Dec 2025. Targets on the main list — Frontier → Verizon, Quantum Fiber → AT&T, Metronet → T-Mobile/KKR, Ziply → BCE, Consolidated → Searchlight, Windstream ↔ Uniti, Google Fiber → Stonepeak/Astound (pending) — appear in their own rows above.

Method

  • Source: FCC Broadband Data Collection fixed availability, as-of 12/31/2025 (July 2026 public release), technology code 50 (fiber to the premises), all 50 states + DC — 71.3M fiber-passed BSLs, 1,570 fiber filers.
  • Passings basis: distinct broadband-serviceable locations. A multi-unit building counts as one BSL, so MDU-heavy footprints (Hotwire, Verizon, Blue Stream) show fewer BSLs than their marketed unit passings.
  • Overlap: another distinct filing provider (any of the 1,570, not just these fourteen) reporting technology 50 at the same location_id in the same vintage. Keyed by provider_id, never brand.
  • Entity notes: Windstream = all Kinetic subsidiaries plus Uniti entities (one filing ID). Metronet includes Vexus. Lumen = Quantum Fiber + CenturyLink brands. altafiber excludes sister company Hawaiian Telcom (254k BSLs, same filing ID). Consolidated = Fidium's filing entity only.
  • Acquired cohort: screened to targets with 100k+ residential-capable fiber BSLs at Dec 2025; deal window Sept 2022 – Aug 2026 by announce or close date; majority-control changes only. Lumos = provider ID 130403 filtered to Lumos-branded records (the same ID also files T-Mobile Fiber). Empire Access = its filing entity (Empire Long Distance), which includes sister brands. Pending deals (the three T-Mobile JVs) are marked in the table.
  • Caveats: availability is provider-reported and can overstate marketable footprint. On open-access networks (e.g. UTOPIA in Utah) each retail ISP counts as a separate overlapper, which inflates 2+ counts in those areas.
